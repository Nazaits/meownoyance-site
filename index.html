Meownoyance — Privacy Policy

Effective 17 August 2026

The short version: Meownoyance does not collect, store remotely, share, or sell any data. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads — and it does not request network access, so it is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere.

What Meownoyance is

Meownoyance is an app that shows an animated cat over apps you choose, to interrupt endless scrolling. Everything it does happens on your device.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Usage Access — used only to know which app is currently on your screen, so the cat can appear in the apps you picked (and nowhere else). Meownoyance reads the name of the foreground app; it does not read anything inside that app — not your feed, messages, or input. This information is processed in the moment, on your device, and is never transmitted, logged for later, or shared.
  • Display over other apps — used to draw the cat on your screen. The overlay shows the cat; it does not capture or record what is beneath it.
  • Notifications — used for one persistent notification while the cat is on duty, which also contains the stop switch.
  • App list — Settings lists the apps installed on your device solely so you can pick which ones the cat visits. The list is displayed and never leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Your settings (chosen apps, schedules, dials), your cats and their names, and your collection progress are stored in the app's private storage on your device only. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What we collect

Nothing. Meownoyance contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting service, and no third-party code that phones home. The app does not request the Internet permission, so no data can leave your device through it.

Children

Meownoyance is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above — for example, adding optional in-app purchases through Google Play — this policy will be updated first, and the "effective" date above will change. Purchases, if added, would be processed by Google Play; Meownoyance itself would still not collect personal data.
